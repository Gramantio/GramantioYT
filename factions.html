Military Factions

Explore the factions of United Factions, their battlefield doctrines, and the forces they bring to the war.

Global Power · Advanced Warfare

United States

The United States relies on advanced military technology, precision firepower, and powerful air support. Its forces excel at engaging enemies from a distance, but maintaining its expensive arsenal requires careful resource management.

Global Power · Heavy Warfare

China

China fields heavy armor, powerful weapons, and large formations of combat units. Its strength comes from overwhelming enemy positions with a combination of durability, firepower, and numbers.

Global Power · Asymmetric Warfare

Global Liberation Army

The GLA uses unconventional tactics, flexible forces, and resourceful battlefield strategies. Its units can pressure enemy positions through mobility, ambushes, and sustained attacks rather than relying entirely on expensive equipment.

Independent Power · Combined Warfare

HALO

HALO combines and adapts military knowledge from the world's major powers to create its own battlefield doctrine. Its different force paths specialize in distinct approaches, ranging from advanced technology to heavy firepower and large-scale guerrilla warfare.
